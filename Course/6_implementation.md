# 7. The Implementation

The *how* of our GPIO application is described by some implementation. The
creation of one such implementation is not within the scope of this course, as
it involves a heavy dose of macro magic and fancy C. It is, however an
interesting topic and one you undoubtedly will encounter if working with
zephyr. Refer to the *Next Steps* section of the overview to find some
resources describing driver implementation.
